This is the first main iteration of this project. Attached are stl's for commonly used dock sizes as well as the cable clamps used. Additionally, an Onshape link can be found below which will take you to the file containing the raw CAD files for this design. In here, you can adjust cable diameters for cable clamps and a range of parameters to make this project fit your needs. The parts for this project can be easily 3D printed without any special settings using a 0.4mm nozzle.

3D Printed Parts:
1. Dock - The part the cables sit in when not in use
2. Cable Clamp Top - The top half of the part that clamps the cable which adapts to the dock
3. Cable Clamp Bottom - The bottom half of the part that clamps the cable which adapts to the dock
4. Under-desk Cable Holders - Takes away the sling in cable past the dock, ensures that cables can be pulled out and pushed in easily while not hanging down and getting in the way

Hardware needed to create this:
6x2mm Neodymium Magnets - Quantity = 2 per each cable docked
M2x6mm Socket Head Cap Screw - Quantity = 2 per each cable docked
M2x4mm Heat Threaded Insert - Quantity = 2 per each cable docked
Some double sided tape or Velcro to attach to desk and to the docking system

Instructions:

1. Print a docking station as well as enough top and bottom cable clamps to fill your docking stations. Optionally, you can print the under-desk cable holders to keep your cable held close to your desk instead of slacking down right out of the dock.
2. Add a dab of hot glue or super glue into the circular pockets found in the dock and the bottom cable clamps.
3. Place magnets in the ciruclar pockets found in the dock and the bottom cable clamps so that they attract when they are next to eachother. (Opposite poles touching when dock)
4. Using a soldering iron or heat threaded insert tool, press your heat threaded inserts into the bottom of the bottom cable clamps.
5. Clamp your cables using a top and bottom cable clamp and screw them together using your M2 screws.
6. Use double sided tape or Velcro to secure your cable dock and under-desk cable holders to the underside of your desk where you want them.
7. Feed your cables through the cable holders and dock your cables in your new under-desk cable dock
